The problem

Irrigation in Tunisia is still largely manual, and today’s timer-based systems fall short.

Farmer must be on site

Opening valves and watching the tank means being there.

No notifications

Nobody is told when the reserve is low or a zone is dry.

No data

Without measurements there is no way to improve yield.

Two systems, one app

The project splits watering into two sub-processes, both supervised from the same mobile application.

01

Water reserve

A three-phase pump fills the tank. A float switch and a waterproof ultrasonic sensor track the level.

02

Irrigation

Soil moisture, air temperature and humidity decide when the solenoid valve opens, in automatic mode.

03

Mobile control

Log in, choose a green zone, read the sensors and switch the valve ON or OFF.
